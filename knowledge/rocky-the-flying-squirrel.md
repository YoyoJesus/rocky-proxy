# Rocky the Flying Squirrel, the inspiration for this bot's name

Summarized from Wikipedia on October 26, 2026.

## Who Rocky is
Rocket "Rocky" J. Squirrel, also known as Rocky the Flying Squirrel, is one of the two protagonists of the
1959-1964 animated series Rocky and His Friends and The Bullwinkle Show, often called The Rocky and Bullwinkle
Show. The shows were produced by Jay Ward, and the middle initial "J" given to Rocky and Bullwinkle is a
reference to him. Rocky is the best friend of Bullwinkle J. Moose.

## Creation
Jay Ward and Alex Anderson first created Rocky for The Frostbite Falls Review, a proposed pilot that was never
developed. Rocky then starred in Rocky and His Friends. When the show moved from ABC to NBC it was renamed The
Bullwinkle Show, with Bullwinkle taking the lead because the moose had become more popular.

## Story and catchphrase
Rocky and Bullwinkle share a house in Frostbite Falls, Minnesota, a parody of International Falls, Minnesota.
Their adventures usually involve foiling the schemes of the spies Boris Badenov and Natasha Fatale, and
sometimes their boss, Fearless Leader. Rocky's best-known catchphrase is "Hokey smoke!"

## Personality
Rocky is an upstanding, plucky, slightly naive all-American youth and the brains and straight man of the
"moose and squirrel" duo. He often comes up with creative solutions, but he falls for Boris and Natasha's
disguises as easily as Bullwinkle does, managing at most "Those two look familiar!"

## Flying
Rocky flies with a jet engine sound effect and was introduced as "that Jet Age aerial ace." Some episodes limit
him to gliding like a real flying squirrel; others show Superman-like flight, such as flying from Minnesota to
Washington, D.C. He learned to fly at the Cedar Yorpantz Flying School, a play on "seat of your pants."

## Voice actors
June Foray voiced Rocky (and Natasha Fatale) from the original series through the 2000 film until she retired
in 2014. Tara Strong voiced Rocky in the 2018 Amazon series The Adventures of Rocky and Bullwinkle. In the 1992
film Boris and Natasha: The Movie, Andrea Martin played Rocky as a human woman, "Agent Squirrel."

## Other appearances
Rocky appeared in Gary Larson's The Far Side, a 2014 GEICO commercial with the GEICO Gecko, and comics from
Dell, Gold Key, Charlton, Star Comics, Marvel and IDW. Video games include The Adventures of Rocky and
Bullwinkle and Friends (1992) and Rocky and Bullwinkle (2008).
